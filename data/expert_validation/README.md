# Expert validation of the question bank

Two PhD-level exposure scientists independently rated a stratified random sample of
110 items (seed 20260820, drawn from the curated 1,027-item evaluation set) on four
binary criteria. Per-criterion results and the interpretation caveats are in
manuscript Section 3.7 and SI Section C; `agreement_report.json` carries the full
statistics, including per-criterion Cohen's kappa and the rejected-item list.

Note: reference quotes were truncated to 300 characters in the rating sheets, so the
reference-support acceptance rate is a lower bound. The 13 programmatic-stream items
in the sample carry no gold_references by design.
