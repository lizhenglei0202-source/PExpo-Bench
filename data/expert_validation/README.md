# Expert validation of the question bank

A stratified random re-audit of 110 items was performed by two PhD-level exposure
scientists under a 4-of-4 acceptance criterion (question clarity, gold correctness,
reference support, difficulty appropriateness), yielding the acceptance rate reported
in the manuscript (Section 3.7) and the protocol described in SI Section C.

The per-rater rating sheets and the rejected-item resolution log are held by the
authors and are available on request, alongside the raw pre-correction trajectories
(see the Data Availability statement).
