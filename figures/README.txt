Figure files and their numbering in the paper
=============================================
The filenames below predate the final figure ordering. The paper's numbering is
authoritative; build_docx.py maps each file to its correct caption.

  Figure_2_results.png                   -> Figure 2  (accuracy, sub-domain, accuracy-cost)
  Figure_4_reliability_diagnostics.png   -> Figure 3  (instruction following, coverage,
                                                      contradiction)
  Figure_5_configuration_contrast.png    -> Figure 4  (A4 vs A3 contrasts and heterogeneity)

SUPPLEMENTARY  (note: filename number != SI figure number)
